Holiday decorating

Christmas, handled.

I design it. My team installs it. You get the house you wanted without spending a single Saturday on a ladder.

Book your holiday install

What this is

A designed holiday,
not a decorating package.

There’s a version of holiday decorating where somebody shows up with a tree, a box of ornaments, and the same plan they used at four other houses that week. This isn’t that.

I start with your house. The scale of the rooms, the colors you already live with, where people gather, what the light does at night. Then I design a holiday scheme that belongs in that house and nowhere else.

Some of my clients want the whole home. Some want the entry and the great room done properly and nothing else. Both are real projects and I treat them the same way.

How it works

Three steps,
and two of them are mine.

  1. 01

    We talk

    I come see the house, hear what you’re picturing, and get a feel for how you use the space over the holidays.

  2. 02

    I design it

    Scheme, palette, scale, room by room. You see the plan before anything is ordered or installed.

  3. 03

    My team installs it

    We handle the setup so you don’t have to be there for any of it.

What gets decorated

As much or as little
as you want.

Trees, tabletop to two storeys. Staircases and railings. Mantels and fireplaces. Entries and front doors. Dining tables set for the season. Guest rooms, if you’re hosting. Tell me the rooms that matter to you and we’ll start there.

Why a designer

The reason it looks expensive
is that it was designed.

More about me

I’ve spent more than thirty years doing residential interior design, working through every part of the process from the first idea to the final install. That’s the same eye and the same coordination, applied to five weeks of the year instead of five months.

It shows up in the small things. Palettes that hold together from room to room, and scale that’s right in a space with twenty-foot ceilings. Most of my holiday clients call me back the next year rather than starting over with someone else.
