Owner, Pam Hassler Interiors

Pam Hassler

Since 2020, I have owned and operated Pam Hassler Interiors, specializing in residential interior design and holiday decorating. With more than 30 years of experience in the interior design industry, I have had the privilege of helping homeowners create beautiful, inviting spaces that truly reflect their personal style and lifestyle.

I have lived and worked in South Florida for many years, giving me a unique understanding of the area’s architecture, lifestyle, and ever-changing design trends. I have watched South Florida design evolve over the decades and have developed a strong appreciation for the way our climate, natural surroundings, and relaxed yet sophisticated lifestyle influence the way we live and design our homes.

I bring that knowledge and experience to every project, creating interiors that feel current and fresh while remaining timeless and personal.

Throughout my career, I have been fortunate to work on every aspect of the residential design process, from the initial vision and space planning through selections, sourcing, and final installation. I truly enjoy collaborating with my clients and the many talented professionals involved in bringing a project to life.

My hands-on approach, attention to detail, and ability to coordinate all of the moving pieces allow me to create a seamless experience while ensuring that the finished home feels cohesive, comfortable, and uniquely theirs.
