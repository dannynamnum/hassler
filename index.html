Seasonal

And then there is Christmas.

See the holiday work

Every December I turn the same process on the season. Most holiday decorating comes off a shelf and gets repeated house to house. I design yours the way I would design a room, around your architecture and the way your family actually uses the place. Then my team does the work.

  1. 01

    We talk

    I come see the house, hear what you’re picturing, and get a feel for how you use the space over the holidays.

  2. 02

    I design it

    Scheme, palette, scale, room by room. You see the plan before anything is ordered or installed.

  3. 03

    My team installs it

    We handle the setup so you don’t have to be there for any of it.
