The service

You leave for the day.
You come back to Christmas.

I don’t arrive with one look and fit your house to it. I start with your rooms, your colors and the way you actually live, then build the season around that.

  1. 01

    Design

    I walk the house first. Then I put together a scheme for the rooms you actually use, in colors that sit with what you already own.

  2. 02

    Install

    My team brings everything and sets it in one visit. Trees, mantels, garland, the table, the lights outside.

  3. 03

    Takedown

    We come back in January, take it all down and pack it properly, so next season starts from order and not from a pile of boxes.
